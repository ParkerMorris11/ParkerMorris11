## Featured work

### [AUP Mobilization Flow](https://github.com/ParkerMorris11/aup-mobilization-flow)

An executive-sponsored AI policy activation product developed during my AI Adoption internship at BrainStorm.

- Built with the Claude API, Next.js, and TypeScript
- Used structured outputs, source-grounded extraction and generation, semantic matching, and validation safeguards
- Reduced policy mobilization time by **88%**, from approximately 2 hours to 15 minutes
- Piloted with **80+ employees** and evaluated with a municipal customer
- Transitioned the validated prototype to Product and Engineering for continued development

### [AI Digest Agent](https://github.com/ParkerMorris11/ai-digest)

A self-hosted Python and Claude agent that turns four AI newsletters into a personalized email briefing.

- Aggregates and analyzes newsletter content
- Deduplicates stories, scores relevance, and tags categories
- Generates “why it matters” summaries and suggested action items
- Delivers a formatted HTML briefing in approximately 30 seconds per run
